# ASDE Phase 4 — Formal Mathematical & Physical Models

## Objective
Develop explicit competing probability and physical models, derive
their consequences, verify algebra computationally and subject them
to adversarial review.

## Work packages
1. Formal foundations and assumptions ledger
2. CHSH baseline derivation and assumption audit
3. Measurement dependence and probability constraints
4. Competing model classes and explicit toy constructions
5. Parameter constraints, identifiability and sensitivity
6. Independent mathematical challenge and counterexample search
7. Phase synthesis and decision gate

## Scientific integrity
The CHSH deterministic baseline is checked by exhaustive enumeration.
The quantum singlet baseline is checked numerically. Neither establishes
a superdeterministic physical model. LLM-generated analyses are research
leads, not proofs or independent replications.
